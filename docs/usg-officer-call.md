# What a USG Funding officer told us (Nov 14, 2026)

For slides, Devpost and the demo video. She asked **not to be quoted or named**: say "we spoke with a USG Funding Department officer," and paraphrase. No direct quotes.

## What she said
- **The guidelines are the main problem.** The rules aren't changing; clubs don't check them. When an application follows the guidelines, a denial is very rare: USG wants to fund everyone.
- **Most common reasons a line can't be funded:** submitted under 6 weeks before the event; a vendor that isn't on USG's approved list (her recent example was a printing service, so it's not only food); talent or performers; durable goods (reusable items); items for staff.
- **One thing wrong doesn't mean denial.** USG emails the club to fix it, and funds it once fixed. That email back-and-forth, and how fast the club replies, is the real delay. Hours to a week; there's no average.
- **Review team per fund:** a director, an associate director and a delegate check each application against a checklist. No voting. Every application gets the same review whether it's $500 or $3,000.
- **Limits she stated:** up to $4,000 a semester, $8,000 a year per club. (The $4,000 semester limit isn't in the written guidelines we have; Greenlight shows it as an amber check.)
- **Status updates aren't a pain point.** USG already emails clubs at each step.
- **Her top wish:** technology that knows the guidelines, so a club can't submit until everything passes ("fix A, fix B, then submit"). Automation would mainly make review faster.

## What we built because of it
| She said | Greenlight now |
|---|---|
| Clubs don't check the guidelines | Submit stays locked until every rule passes; "Fix these first" links each item to its field |
| Unapproved vendors, including printing | Every non-food purchase needs a named vendor the club confirms is USG-approved (food vendors are checked against USG's list) |
| USG emails a fix instead of denying | Reviewer "Send back to fix": tick the problems, the club gets a numbered list by email and on its request, each item crossed off as it's fixed |
| Clean applications should go fast | Fast lane: applications with nothing to fix or check go to the top of the queue; one click fills the full amount and the email; a person still presses Send |
| $4,000 a semester | Amber check that cites the conversation, not a block, until confirmed in writing |
